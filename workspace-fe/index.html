Capabilities

Everything a product team argues about — settled with evidence.

Six focused workflows that replace the Friday-afternoon scramble across docs, dashboards, and threads.

Instant briefs

Turn a fuzzy request into a one-pager: problem, evidence, sizing, risks, and open questions — drafted in under a minute.

Signal hub

Connect Linear, Jira, Zendesk, Amplitude, and Gong. Nebula clusters raw feedback into themes with frequency and revenue weight.

Decision memory

Every call is archived with its rationale and sources, so new hires can ask "why is it like this?" and get a real answer.

Research synthesis

Drop in 30 interview transcripts. Get themes, verbatim quotes, and confidence bands — with every claim linked back to the timestamp.

Roadmap forecasting

Model the impact of moving a bet up or down. Nebula estimates reach, effort, and risk from your own historical delivery data.

Enterprise controls

SSO/SAML, granular roles, audit logs, and zero-training data isolation. Your workspace data never leaves your tenancy.

How it works

Live in an afternoon, not a quarter.

No data-model migration, no prompt-engineering course. Three steps from signup to your first shipped decision.

  1. 01

    Connect your stack

    OAuth into the tools you already use. Nebula indexes history read-only and keeps permissions mirroring your source systems.

  2. 02

    Ask in plain language

    "What's blocking enterprise expansion?" gets a ranked answer with charts, quotes, and citations — not a generic chatbot essay.

  3. 03

    Ship with confidence

    Export a decision doc to Notion or Slack, assign the follow-up, and let Nebula track whether the outcome matched the bet.
